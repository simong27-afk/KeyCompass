## What we do

We offer two services.

**Private onboarding session.** Ninety minutes to a day, live and one-to-one. Choosing and
checking a hardware wallet, setting it up from scratch, generating and backing up a recovery
phrase, sending a test amount, confirming it arrived, then moving the balance. You perform
every step yourself, on your own devices, with each step explained as you go.

**Threat and security review.** A structured review of a setup you already have, across four
failure channels: phishing, device, backup loss, and succession. Nothing is changed during a
review. You receive a written, prioritised list of what to fix worst-first, plain-English
reasoning for each item, and an explicit list of what is already sound.

Both begin with a free fifteen-minute intake call, at no charge and no obligation. If you do
not need the service yet, we will tell you so.

After a session or review you receive a written summary within 48 hours covering what was
done and what remains outstanding, and one follow-up thread by email for the first month.

Sessions run over video call and are not recorded. Screen sharing runs on our side only.

how to hold safely what you already own. We explain, demonstrate and review. You choose, you
act, and you remain solely responsible at all times for your keys, your backups, your devices
and your funds.

With less than 48 hours' notice, or where an appointment is missed without notice, the session
