## Verifying that it is really us

This section matters more than the addresses above. Read it before you reply to anything
claiming to come from KeyCompass.

- **KeyCompass never makes contact first.** No cold call or phone call, ever. No unexpected
  email, no message on Telegram or WhatsApp. Every conversation starts with you booking it.
  Anyone approaching you out of the blue claiming to be Simon is not Simon.
- **Only two addresses are ever used:** `hello@keycompass.co.uk` and
  `simon@keycompass.co.uk`. Read the address character by character before you reply.
  Lookalike domains cost a few pounds, and a single swapped letter is the oldest trick in
  this business.
- **The answer to "send me your recovery phrase" is always no.** Not to KeyCompass, not to
  a support agent, not to a wallet app, not to anyone, ever. Someone asking is someone
  stealing from you, and stopping mid-sentence is always the right call.

## What happens next

Every engagement begins with a free fifteen-minute intake call. There is no obligation and
no pitch. If KeyCompass is not what you need, you will be told so on the call.

Prices are per engagement and confirmed in writing before anything is booked. Reviews are
scoped on that intake call: a single wallet with a single backup sits at the bottom of the
range; multiple devices, multisig, or a business setup sits at the top.
