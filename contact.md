## Verifying that it is really us

This section matters more than the addresses above. Read it before you reply to anything
claiming to come from KeyCompass.

- **KeyCompass never contacts you first about your crypto.** We never phone you first.
  No unexpected email, no message on Telegram, WhatsApp or LinkedIn. Every conversation about
  your own crypto, or an estate, starts with you booking it. Anyone approaching you out of the
  blue about your crypto and claiming to be Simon is not Simon.
- **The one exception is professional firms.** KeyCompass may write to solicitors, will
  writers and other professional firms, by post or by email to the firm, to offer a free
  briefing for their team, and may phone the firm's office to follow up a letter already
  sent. These approaches never mention a client, never ask for anything sensitive, and are
  never made by LinkedIn message.
- **Only one phone number is ours:** +44 1603 381989. If a call claims to be from KeyCompass,
  hang up and call that number back yourself. Caller ID can be faked; a number you dial
  yourself cannot.
- **Only one LinkedIn page is KeyCompass:**
  [linkedin.com/company/keycompass](https://www.linkedin.com/company/keycompass), alongside
  Simon's own profile at
  [uk.linkedin.com/in/simongeils](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/simongeils). A page or profile
  using the KeyCompass name that is not one of those two is not KeyCompass, however
  convincing the logo looks.
- **Only two addresses are ever used:** `hello@keycompass.co.uk` and
  `simon@keycompass.co.uk`. Read the address character by character before you reply.
  Lookalike domains cost a few pounds, and a single swapped letter is the oldest trick in
  this business.
- **The answer to "send me your recovery phrase" is always no.** Not to KeyCompass, not to
  a support agent, not to a wallet app, not to anyone, ever. Someone asking is someone
  stealing from you, and stopping mid-sentence is always the right call.
