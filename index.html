You will always know what happens next.

Questions The ones worth asking first

Before you book.

Will you ever ask for my recovery phrase?

No. Not the words, not a photo, not "just the first four". I will never see your phrase, your keys, or your funds, and there is no situation in which I would need to. If anyone ever asks you for it — including someone claiming to be me — that is the scam, and that is the value here.

How do I know it’s really you?

I never make contact first. No cold call or phone call, ever. No unexpected email, no message on Telegram or WhatsApp. Every conversation starts with you booking it — so anyone approaching you out of the blue claiming to be me is not me.

I write from two addresses: hello@keycompass.co.uk & simon@keycompass.co.uk. Read it character by character before you reply. Lookalike domains cost a few pounds, a single swapped letter is the oldest trick in this business, and a convincing message from a near-miss address is still a scam.

And whoever appears to be asking, the answer to “send me your recovery phrase” is no. Not to me, not to support or suggested app, not to anyone, ever. Someone asking is someone stealing from you, and stopping mid-sentence is always the right call.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. KeyCompass is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and does not give investment, legal, or tax advice. I will not tell you what to hold, when to buy, or how much. Every session is about the technical and operational side of holding what you already own. For investment, legal, and tax decisions, use your own licensed professionals.

Is this Bitcoin only?

No. The work covers most networks and the wallets that suppoort them, rather than one chain. Multi-asset setups are usually where the interesting failure modes live, so they are squarely in scope for a review. We will also strive to seek out setups that work with the more nuanced and new projects, ensuring we get the right setup that works for you.

I am not technical. Is that a problem?

Most clients are not. This is the value proposition. Nothing in a session assumes prior knowledge, nothing is skipped because it seems obvious, and there is no question too basic to ask. I am highly skilled in breaking down complex concepts into easily understandable guidance. If a session ends with you nodding along and not understanding, it has failed.

Where are you, and can we work together?

UK-based, working remotely with clients anywhere. Sessions run over video call, scheduled in your timezone wherever the hours allow.

Can you help with inheritance planning or a business treasury?

Not yet, and I would rather say that plainly than take the booking. This is something I am currently working on and plan to open up shortly. A security review can tell you today where your succession plan stands. If you need the full setup now, say so and I will gauge what we can and can't plan together, today.
