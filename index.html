How a session runs Four steps, in order

You will always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Before — the free 15 minutes

    A short intake form, then a fifteen-minute call at no charge. We work out which service you actually need and what it will cost. If the answer is that you do not need this yet, I will say so.

  2. 02

    Prep — a list, sent in advance

    What to have ready: which devices, a quiet hour, a pen. Nothing sensitive gets written down or photographed ahead of time, and nothing gets sent to me.

  3. 03

    The session — video call

    Screen sharing runs on my side only. You never show me a recovery phrase, a private key, or an unlocked wallet, and I will stop you if you start to. Everything sensitive stays on your side of the call.

  4. 04

    After — you get it in writing

    A written summary within 48 hours covering what you set up and what is left to do, plus one follow-up thread for anything that comes up in the first month.

Questions The ones worth asking first

Before you book.

Will you ever ask for my recovery phrase?

No. Not the words, not a photo, not "just the first four". I will never see your phrase, your keys, or your funds, and there is no situation in which I would need to. If anyone ever asks you for it — including someone claiming to be me — that is the scam, and that is the whole lesson.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. KeyCompass is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and does not give investment, legal, or tax advice. I will not tell you what to hold, when to buy, or how much. Every session is about the technical and operational side of holding what you already own. For investment, legal, and tax decisions, use your own licensed professionals.

Is this Bitcoin only?

No. The work covers the major assets and the wallets that hold them, rather than one chain. Multi-asset setups are usually where the interesting failure modes live, so they are squarely in scope for a review.

I am not technical. Is that a problem?

Most clients are not. That is the job. Nothing in a session assumes prior knowledge, nothing is skipped because it seems obvious, and there is no question too basic to ask. If a session ends with you nodding along and not understanding, it has failed.

Where are you, and can we work together?

UK-based, working remotely with clients anywhere. Sessions run over video call, scheduled in your timezone wherever the hours allow.

Can you help with inheritance planning or a business treasury?

Not yet, and I would rather say that plainly than take the booking. Both touch outcomes that cannot be undone, so neither opens until professional indemnity insurance and solicitor-drafted engagement terms are confirmed in place. A security review can tell you today where your succession plan stands. If you need the full setup now, say so on the intake call and I will point you elsewhere.
