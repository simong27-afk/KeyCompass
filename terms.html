What we do

We offer two services.

Private onboarding session. Ninety minutes to a day, live and one-to-one. Choosing and checking a hardware wallet, setting it up from scratch, generating and backing up a recovery phrase, sending a test amount, confirming it arrived, then moving the balance. You perform every step yourself, on your own devices, with each step explained as you go.

Threat and security review. A structured review of a setup you already have, across four failure channels: phishing, device, backup loss, and succession. Nothing is changed during a review. You receive a written, prioritised list of what to fix worst-first, plain-English reasoning for each item, and an explicit list of what is already sound.

Both begin with a free fifteen-minute intake call, at no charge and no obligation. If you do not need the service yet, we will tell you so.

After a session or review you receive a written summary within 48 hours covering what was done and what remains outstanding, and one follow-up thread by email for the first month.

Sessions run over video call and are not recorded. Screen sharing runs on our side only.

The boundary — what KeyCompass is not

This section is the most important part of these terms.

We are not a custodian. We never take custody, control or possession of your cryptoassets, your private keys, your recovery phrase or your wallet passwords — not at any point, not temporarily, and not to check or assist with something. Your assets stay in your sole control throughout.

We never ask for your recovery phrase, private keys or wallet passwords. Not the words, not a photograph, not part of them. There is no circumstance in which we would need them. Anyone asking you for them — including someone presenting themselves as KeyCompass — is not acting for us.

We are not a financial adviser and we are not regulated. KeyCompass is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. Nothing we provide is investment, legal or tax advice, a personal recommendation, or an opinion on what you should buy, sell or hold, or when. Cryptoassets are unregulated and high risk. You could lose all the money you invest. You should take independent professional advice before making investment, legal or tax decisions.

You make every decision and perform every action. Our work is technical and operational: how to hold safely what you already own. We explain, demonstrate and review. You choose, you act, and you remain solely responsible at all times for your keys, your backups, your devices and your funds.

How an engagement is agreed

  1. You complete a short intake form and book the free fifteen-minute call.
  2. On that call we establish which service fits and what it will involve.
  3. We send you a written scope and quotation.
  4. You accept it in writing and book a time. At that point these terms apply and the engagement is agreed.

We do not publish prices, because both services are scoped to the setup in front of us. A single wallet with a single backup sits at the bottom of the range; multiple devices, multisig, or a business setup sits at the top. You will always have the price in writing before you commit to anything.

We may decline an engagement, or stop one, where we judge we cannot carry it out safely, competently or lawfully. If we do that before the work starts, you pay nothing.

What we need from you

You agree to:

  • give us accurate and complete information about your setup, so that what we tell you is based on what you actually have;
  • have the devices, accounts and materials listed in the prep note ready before the session;
  • attend at the time booked, or tell us in advance if you cannot;
  • never disclose your recovery phrase, private keys or wallet passwords to us or to anyone else, in any format, for any reason;
  • never display a recovery phrase, private key or unlocked wallet on camera or on a shared screen; and
  • carry out any steps we recommend yourself, and keep your own records and backups of what you set up.

If the information you give us is inaccurate or incomplete, or you do not have what the prep note asks for, a session may run short or a review may reach the wrong conclusion. We are not responsible for that outcome.

Fees and payment

Fees are quoted per engagement, in writing, before you book. The quotation states what is included.

KeyCompass is not currently registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged on our fees. The price you are quoted is the price you pay.

Payment is due in full before the session. When you accept the scope and quotation we send an invoice with bank transfer details, and your time is confirmed once payment has been received.

Where you are a business client and payment is late, we may charge interest and recovery costs under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. This does not apply to consumers.

Cancelling, rescheduling and refunds

If you are a consumer, you have a statutory right to cancel. Because you book at a distance, you may cancel within 14 days of the engagement being agreed, without giving a reason, and receive a refund.

Sessions are often booked and delivered inside that 14-day window. If you want the work to start before the 14 days are up, you must ask us to begin — booking a time inside the window is that request. Two things follow, and by booking you acknowledge both:

  • once the service has been fully performed, your right to cancel is gone; and
  • if you cancel part-way through, you pay a proportionate amount for what has already been provided.

To cancel, email hello@keycompass.co.uk. You may use the model cancellation form in the Consumer Contracts Regulations, but you do not have to. Refunds are made within 14 days of us being told, by the method you paid with.

Separately from the statutory right above, and without reducing it, our own rescheduling policy is this. You may reschedule once at no charge, provided you give us at least 48 hours' notice. With less than 48 hours' notice, or where an appointment is missed without notice, the session is treated as delivered and the fee remains payable. If we have to reschedule, you choose between a new time and a full refund.

Your report and who owns it

You may use the written summary or review report we produce for you, for your own purposes, indefinitely. That includes showing it to your own advisers, your family or your colleagues.

We keep ownership of what sits underneath it: our methods, templates, checklists and the way we structure a review. You may not republish, resell or distribute our materials publicly, or use them to provide a similar service to others.

A review is a point-in-time assessment. It describes your setup as it stood on the day, on the information you gave us. Setups drift and threats change; a report does not stay accurate by itself.

We treat what we learn about your setup and your circumstances as confidential, and do not disclose it to anyone except where you ask us to, or where the law requires it. We ask the same of you in respect of our materials and methods.

Sessions are not recorded. Screen sharing runs on our side only, so nothing sensitive on your side of the call is transmitted.

We handle your personal information as a data controller, as described in our privacy notice. We never ask for, receive or store your recovery phrase, your private keys or your wallet passwords.
