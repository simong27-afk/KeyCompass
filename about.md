# About KeyCompass

> Independent self-custody onboarding and cryptoasset security reviews. UK-based, working
> remotely with clients anywhere. KeyCompass never holds your keys, your recovery phrase,
> or your funds.

KeyCompass is a solo practice run by Simon Geils. It does one thing: it helps people hold
their own cryptoassets safely, either by setting a wallet up with them from scratch or by
reviewing a setup they already have.

There is no product to sell, no affiliate link, no opinion on what anyone should buy. The
work is technical and operational — how to hold safely what you already own.

## Who you are talking to

Over a decade in front-line technical support, including experience at Ledger and Apple,
helping individuals and businesses set up and maintain their digital assets, recover them,
and secure what was on them. Thousands of conversations, and the same handful of mistakes
appearing over and over.

KeyCompass is that pattern turned into a service. Not a wealth manager who also does
crypto — the person who has sat with someone on the worst day of their financial life and
worked out, step by step, what happened.

## The boundary

This is the part worth reading twice, because it is what makes the work safe to do with
someone you have just met.

- **Not a custodian.** KeyCompass never holds your keys, your recovery phrase, or your
  funds — not for a minute, not to help, not to check something. You keep sole control of
  everything, throughout every engagement.
- **Not a broker.** Nothing is bought, sold, or exchanged on your behalf.
- **Not an adviser.** Nothing said in a session is investment, legal, or tax advice. No one
  will tell you what to buy, when to buy, or how much.

KeyCompass is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. For
investment, legal, and tax decisions, use your own licensed professionals.

## How the work runs

Every engagement starts with a free fifteen-minute intake call to work out which service
you actually need and what it will cost. If the answer is that you do not need this yet,
you will be told so on that call.

Sessions run over video call. Screen sharing runs on the practitioner's side only. You
never show a recovery phrase, a private key, or an unlocked wallet — and you will be
stopped if you start to. Everything sensitive stays on your side of the call.

## What is not offered

Inheritance planning and full business treasury setup are not offered yet. A security
review can tell you today where your succession plan stands, but the complete service is
still in development, and saying so plainly is better than taking the booking.
